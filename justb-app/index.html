01 QUICK START

Play your first game in two minutes. Grab a 21-card deck, find a friend, go.

  1. GET YOUR DECK

    One 21-card deck with at least 4 Fighters. A Booster Pack is a full deck, ready to play.

  2. FILL THE POT

    Each player puts a B collectible, or an agreed amount of Sats, in the middle. Knockout takes it all.

  3. SHUFFLE AND DRAW 5

    Your hand is 5 cards. The rest stays face down as your Stack. Your Stack is your life. Flip a coin for who goes first, that player skips the first Fuel flip.

  4. PLAY YOUR TURN

    Draw, flip a Fuel card, deploy a Fighter, attack, play a Note if you want, pass. Under a minute once you know it.

  5. COMPARE POWER

    Higher Power wins the clash. The loser discards 1 card from their Stack. A tie does nothing.

  6. KNOCK THEM OUT

    Your opponent runs out of cards in their Stack? You win and take the Pot.

Example round

B-Lightning (Power 7) deploys and equips Cold Storage for +2, so it attacks at 9. B-Bullish answers at 8. Nine beats eight, B-Bullish's player discards 1 card. Easy.

Heads up

If you draw a Trap, it hits you right away. Do what the card says and keep playing.

06 DECK BUILDING

Exactly 21 cards. No more, no less. One legal way to fill all 21 slots:

6 Fighters 9 Fuel 2 Notes 2 Gear 1 Energy 1 Trap
  • Copy limits. Up to 4 of any Street or Rare card. Only 1 of any Ultra Rare.
  • Mystery cards. At most 1 per deck, and only if you actually own one. It never comes from a regular pack, only a Just Box blind pull.
  • Minimum Fighters. 4, or the deck isn't legal for match play.
  • Traps. Optional. Run 0 to 2 if you want the extra risk.
  • The Pot. Stake goes in the middle before anyone shuffles.
07 TURN STRUCTURE

Six beats, same every turn. Once players know their cards a full turn runs under a minute. The game is built to move fast.

DRAW

One card from your deck. Draw a Trap and it hits you right away.

FUEL

Flip the top card face up. Sats bank up turn after turn until you spend them.

DEPLOY

Play a Fighter or attach Gear. Discard Sats equal to its cost.

ATTACK

Fire your Move. Compare Power against the opponent's Fighter.

NOTE

Either player can answer with an instant-speed Note.

PASS

Turn ends. Back to draw.

08 HOW YOU WIN
KNOCKOUT

One path to victory. Drop your opponent's Stack to zero through Fighter clashes. Lose a clash, discard 1 card. Run out and you're out. No life totals to track, ever.

The Pot

Before anyone shuffles, both players put something in the middle: a B collectible, or an agreed amount of Sats. One or the other, never a card from your deck. The Knockout winner takes it all. Play for fun or play for keeps, agree the stakes first.

13 FAQ

The "wait, can I…?" questions, answered.

Can I play a Mystery B in my deck?
Yes, max 1 per deck, and only if you own one. It never comes from a regular pack, only from a Just Box.
What happens when Power is tied?
Nothing. Nobody discards a card and the game moves on.
Do my Sats run out at the end of the turn?
No. Sats never expire. Once flipped they stay face up in your Fuel zone until you spend them.
I flipped a Fighter or Note as Fuel. Now what?
It sits face up in your Fuel zone and you can play it from there. It uses a Fuel slot until you do, so too many unplayed ones can leave you short on Sats.
Does the first player flip Fuel?
Not on the first turn. The player going first skips their first Fuel flip.
How many Fighters can be active?
One at a time, unless a card says otherwise.
Do Traps count toward my Fighter or copy limits?
No. Run 0 to 2 Traps per deck. A Trap hits you the moment you draw it.
Do I need a rare card to play?
Never. Street cards are always available and every card plays the same. Rarity only changes how many copies exist.

MYSTERY B

Six B's you can always meet. Then there's the other lot. A Mystery B doesn't sit in the lineup, doesn't get a photo, doesn't get announced. It just turns up in somebody's box, and that somebody has a very good day.

And it never stops. There is no single Mystery B — new ones keep coming, one after another, for as long as the brand keeps drawing. Endless characters, finite supply. Every run is capped, every piece is numbered, and when a run sells out that B never comes back.
