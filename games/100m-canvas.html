ABOUT THE 100M CANVAS

Why 100M?

100 MILLION PIXELS

100 million satoshis = 1 Bitcoin. This canvas is a living demo of Bitcoin's divisibility and the power of micropayments. Each pixel is one sat, so the finished piece is a 1 BTC artwork. Every pixel gets stamped with the Bitcoin block height. Eternal proof of creation.

Nostr for the social layer. Lightning for instant payments. Collaborative art on top. No CEO. No algorithm. No nonsense. Your pixels live on Nostr relays and they're yours forever.
