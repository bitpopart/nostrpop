Johannes Oppewal, pop artist.

Hi, I am Johannes

I am a designer and artist. I paint and design in a thick-outline graffiti pop art style: loud colours, black lines, simple shapes.

POPART.love is where that style meets three plain, good ideas: Love, Freedom and Joy. No complicated story. Just art that is made to be happy.

I like art you can actually hold: cards, prints and frames.

You can find all my art works at bitpopart.com/art, the home of my studio BitPopArt.
