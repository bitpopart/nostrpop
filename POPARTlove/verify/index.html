Verify my card

Check that the number on your card is real and listed by the artist.

Check a serial

Where to find the serial

Numbered cards print their copy number at the bottom right of the front, for example 007/050, and a card code on the back, bottom right, such as K7QM-2XP9. Open edition cards have neither.

What the check does

  1. It checks the number fits the print run of that card.
  2. It looks the number up in the artist's list of sold cards.
  3. It checks the card code printed on the back, a mix of letters and numbers made for that one copy. A made-up serial without the right code fails.

A valid number that is not listed yet may simply not have been recorded. Ask the artist at the next event or through the Artist page.
