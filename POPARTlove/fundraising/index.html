Fundraising

Help POPART grow. Every bit of support turns into more art.

Ways to help

  • Collect. Buy cards and art. It is the best way to keep the studio running.
  • Share. Show a friend a card, post a painting, tell somebody.
  • Subscribe. The newsletter is free and helps plan what to make next.
  • Invite. Bring a PopUp to your market or shop.
