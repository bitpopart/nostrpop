POPPLAY

A two minute card game for two collectors. Collecting stays the main thing.

Bonus game

What is POPPLAY?

A small extra for when two collectors meet. It takes two minutes, with no board and no reading. You only need a stack of cards each. You can play with the cards you have, and if you like, for keeps.

Each card carries a rarity, a tribe and a serial number. Those three things decide who wins a round.

Quick facts

  • 2 players
  • A stack of 5 cards each
  • About 2 minutes
  • Backs face up, nothing to see

How a game goes

  1. Take a stack. Each player holds a stack of 5 cards with the backs facing up. The back shows only the artwork and the name, so nobody knows what is coming. Use cards you can afford to lose if you play for keeps.
  2. Count 3, 2, 1, POP. Both players count out loud and, on POP, flip the top card of their stack face up on the table at the same time. The front shows rarity, tribe and serial.
  3. See who wins. The stronger card wins the round, see the rules below. The winner takes both cards.
  4. Repeat. Play until the stacks are empty. The player with the most cards wins the game. Two players can also play a quick best of 5.

Who wins a round

Check these in order and stop at the first one that decides it.

  1. Rarity. The rarer card wins: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Super Rare, Ultra Rare, Unique.
  2. Tribe. Same rarity? Love beats Freedom, Freedom beats Joy, Joy beats Love.
  3. Serial. Same tribe too? The lower serial wins, so 001 beats 100. An open card loses to a numbered one.
  4. Still equal? Both players take their card back and play the round again.

Example round

Both players flip a card on POP. Paper Plane is Super Rare and Big Heart is Common, so rarity decides it and the Paper Plane wins.

Rules and twists

For fun or for keeps

For fun: everybody gets their own cards back at the end and you only count who won the most rounds.

For keeps: the winner of each round really keeps both cards. Agree on this before you start.

Playing a Trio

Own the same series and the same rarity in all three tribes, Love, Freedom and Joy? Lay all three cards down as one card. A Trio counts as a Unique, so it beats everything except another Trio or a Unique, and then the lower serial wins.

Two extra twists

Streak bonus: win 3 rounds in a row and you also take the top card of your opponent's stack. Then your streak starts again at zero.

One swap: once per game, before you count, say SWAP and trade your top card with the next card in your own stack. You cannot see it first. Each player gets one swap.

Trading still works as before. Same icon swaps 1 for 1, other icons need several cards. See the cards and the trading rules.
