Pick a tribe

Every card carries one theme sign: Love (heart), Freedom (paper plane) or Joy (sun). The sign is your tribe.

The card back is the same for every card, so nobody can see what you hold.

Rare and unique

SeriesWhat it means
MasterpieceThe core of the collection. Bold, clean, collectable.
SignedHand signed by the artist.
LegendRare. Few exist, everybody wants one.
1/1 UniqueOne card, one owner, ever. Sold with a certificate card and can come in a small art frame.

How trading works

  1. Same tribe, fair swap. A card trades one-for-one against a card with the same theme sign.
  2. Other tribes, more cards. To get a card from another tribe, offer more cards. The rarer the card you want, the more cards you need.
  3. Keep the rare ones. Signed, Legend and 1/1 Unique cards are the ones people chase.
Bonus

POPPLAY: the 10-second game

Two players, one stack each. Count down together, 3, 2, 1, POP, and put a card on the table. The higher card wins the round. That is the whole game, so it fits in any pocket and any break.
