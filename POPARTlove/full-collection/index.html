A full collection

What every POPART card adds up to, from one card to the whole set.

The big picture

54 cards make a full collection

POPART cards come in 3 series, 3 tribes and 6 tiers. Own one of every combination and you hold a full collection of 54 cards.

Every card belongs to one series, one tribe and one tier, so you always know where it fits and what is still missing.

The sum

3 x 3 x 6 = 54

  • Series: Masterpiece, Signed, Legend
  • Tribes: Love, Freedom, Joy
  • Tiers: Common up to Unique 1/1

The six tiers and how many exist

These are the starting runs. Each tier is a number you can see on the card, so rarity is never a guess. The last column counts all copies of that tier across the 3 series and 3 tribes, 9 cards per tier.

6,048 numbered copies exist in total, plus the open Commons. Only 9 Unique 1/1 cards will ever exist, one for every series and tribe.

What a collection adds up to

Passport: 18 slots

Your collector passport has one slot for every series and tier, 3 series x 6 tiers. Tick a slot when you own that tier in that series.

Trio: 18 possible

The same series and the same tier in all three tribes make a Trio. There are 18 of them to complete, and a Trio trades for any one card you like.

Series finisher: 3

Fill all 6 tiers of one series in your passport to earn a stamp and ask for the finisher card. Do it three times for all three series.

Collecting in steps

  1. One tribe. Start with the tribe you love most and gather its tiers in one series.
  2. One Trio. Add the same tier from the other two tribes, then trade the three cards for any card you want.
  3. One series. Fill all 6 tiers of a series in your passport and ask for the finisher card.
  4. The full 54. One of every card, all 3 series, all 3 tribes, all 6 tiers.
