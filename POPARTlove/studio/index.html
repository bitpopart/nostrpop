POPART.love is the fan page of the studio BitPopArt.

About BitPopArt

BitPopArt is a pop art studio. It designs and makes loud, happy art in a thick-outline graffiti style: bold colours, black lines and simple shapes, built around three plain, good ideas, Love, Freedom and Joy.

The studio makes art you can hold and share: collectible cards, stickers, prints and digital files you can print at home, plus small tools and games around the art.

What POPART.love is

POPART.love is the fan page for everyone who loves pop art. It is the home of the POPART collectible cards, the Card Maker, stickers, prints, free downloads and news from the studio, so fans can collect, play, download and stay up to date in one place.

More about the artist

Want to know more about the artist behind the studio? You can find the story, the studio and more at bitpopart.com/artist, and all art works at bitpopart.com/art.
