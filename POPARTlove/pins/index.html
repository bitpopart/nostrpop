Pin them on everything

Bag, tee, jacket, cap, lanyard or notebook. Mix a few faces and make your own mood board.

These mockups show the idea. Real pins can look a little different once produced.

The look

Round, shiny and domed like a little button, with the same thick black outline as the rest of the POPART art.

The fit

Planned with a metal clasp so they stay put on fabric and bags. Sizes and materials will be shown with each drop.

The drops

Small batches, a few faces at a time. When a batch is ready it is announced in the news and added to this page.
