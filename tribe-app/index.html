Connect your device

Install or update a TriBe device from your browser with one USB-C cable. About two minutes. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, and a cable that carries data, not a charge-only one.

1Plug in your device
2Press Connect, pick the port
3Choose Update or New
4Press Flash, wait for 100%

Device not listed? Hold BOOT while you plug in the cable, then press Connect again. If an update fails halfway, unplug, hold BOOT, plug in and run New device. That recovers the device. Safari and Firefox cannot flash over USB, because they have no Web Serial. A phone cannot do it either.
