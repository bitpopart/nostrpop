CHARACTER GUIDE

POPtoons are pop art characters by BitPopArt. Each one has a name, a short story and a card. Characters that belong together form a collection.

How the cards work

Every character belongs to one series, one tribe and one tier. The tribe sign decides what a card trades against. The tier says how hard it is to find.
