Pair

Set the clock, change settings, see the battery
Pairing needs Web Bluetooth. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows PC or Android phone (Safari, Firefox and iPhones cannot do this). If you opened it inside a preview frame, open it on its own page.

1. Connect

Hold PWR on the device. It shows READY TO PAIR. (Already running? Tap the red eye on the toon menu to open settings, the device is visible again while that screen is open.) Tap the button, pick POPtoons Eye and press Pair.
