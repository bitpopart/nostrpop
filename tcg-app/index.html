01 QUICK START

Play your first game in two minutes. Grab a 21-card deck, find a friend, go.

  1. GET YOUR DECK

    One 21-card deck with at least 4 Fighters. A Booster Pack is a full deck, ready to play.

  2. FILL THE POT

    Each player puts a B collectible, or an agreed amount of Sats, in the middle. Knockout takes it all.

  3. SHUFFLE AND DRAW 5

    Your hand is 5 cards. The rest stays face down as your Stack. Your Stack is your life. Flip a coin for who goes first, that player skips the first Fuel flip.

  4. PLAY YOUR TURN

    Draw, flip a Fuel card, deploy a Fighter, attack, play a Note if you want, pass. Under a minute once you know it.

  5. COMPARE POWER

    Higher Power wins the clash. The loser discards 1 card from their Stack. A tie does nothing.

  6. KNOCK THEM OUT

    Your opponent runs out of cards in their Stack? You win and take the Pot.

Example round

B-Lightning (Power 7) deploys and equips Cold Storage for +2, so it attacks at 9. B-Bullish answers at 8. Nine beats eight, B-Bullish's player discards 1 card. Easy.

Heads up

If you draw a Trap, it hits you right away. Do what the card says and keep playing.

03 CARD TYPES

Fighters and their Power number are the core. The rest add Fuel, tricks and traps. Every kind has its own colour, so you can call a card from across the table.

B-Fighter

The character. Has Power and one signature Move. Your remaining cards are your life.

Fuel (Sat)

Flip one face up each turn to power your Fighter's Move.

Nostr Note

Instant-speed trick: sabotage, buffs, steals, chaos.

Gear

Equipment that attaches to a Fighter and boosts or changes it.

Energy

Instant-speed power-up you play on your own Fighter for one Move.

Fiat (Brrr)

Trap shuffled into your deck. A hazard that hurts whoever draws it. Follow the card's text, for example discard cards from your own Stack.

Shitcoin (Rug Pull)

Trap shuffled into your deck. Same deal as Fiat, the drawer pays the price. Other traps, like Clownworld Brrr (draw it, discard 2 cards) or CBDC, hurt in their own ways.

Mystery B (Mystery Rare, /21)

The chase card, pulled from a Just Box. Numbered of 21.

Ultra Rare: Gold

Numbered of 100. B-24 Karat and B-Goldrush.

Ultra Rare: Diamonds

Numbered of 100. B-Diamond Hands.

Energy designs so far
  • Diamond Hands. Ignore the next card-loss to your Stack this turn.
  • Power. Return 2 cards from your Discard to your Stack, and your next clash gets +2 Power.
  • Second Wind. Heal 2 cards: take 2 cards from your Discard pile and put them back on your Stack.
Heal = get cards back

In other games this is called "restoring HP". Just B has no life numbers: your cards are your life, so healing simply means returning cards from your Discard pile to your Stack.

05 HOW TO PLAY

The whole game, in four moves.

01

BUILD 21

A 21-card deck with at least 4 Fighters. Start with base Fighters and some Fuel. Notes and Gear are optional.

02

Shuffle, draw a 5-card hand. The rest stays face down as your Stack.

03

CLASH

Each turn: draw, deploy a Fighter, attack. Compare Power. The loser discards 1 card from their Stack.

04

KNOCKOUT

Run out of cards in your Stack and you're done. That's the whole game.

06 DECK BUILDING

Exactly 21 cards. No more, no less. One legal way to fill all 21 slots:

6 Fighters 9 Fuel 2 Notes 2 Gear 1 Energy 1 Trap

The exact mix is yours, as long as you clear 4 Fighters minimum.

  • Copy limits. Up to 4 of any Street or Rare card. Only 1 of any Ultra Rare.
  • Mystery cards. At most 1 per deck, and only if you actually own one. It never comes from a regular pack, only a Just Box blind pull.
  • Minimum Fighters. 4, or the deck isn't legal for match play.
  • Fuel cards. They don't count as a separate resource, they are shuffled into the same 21.
  • Traps. Optional. Run 0 to 2 if you want the extra risk.
  • The Pot. Stake goes in the middle before anyone shuffles.

The Pot

A spot in the middle of the table, and what you play for. Before the first card is drawn, each player puts a B collectible in it. Or you agree on a flat amount of Sats instead, sent to the winner over Lightning. Whoever wins by Knockout takes the whole Pot. Lock it in before Setup step 1, never mid-game.

07 TURN STRUCTURE

Six beats, same every turn. Once players know their cards a full turn runs under a minute. The game is built to move fast.

DRAW

Draw 1 card. A Trap hits you the moment you draw it.

FUEL

Flip 1 card into Fuel. Sats bank until spent.

DEPLOY

Play a Fighter or Gear. Pay Sats equal to its cost.

ATTACK

Your Move fires. Compare Power, the loser discards 1 card from their Stack.

NOTE

Either player may play a Note at instant speed.

PASS

Turn passes, opponent starts back at Draw.

  • Draw. Your only normal draw this turn. If it's a Trap, do what the card says. Clownworld Brrr, for example, makes you discard 2 cards from your own Stack.
  • Fuel. Sats never expire. Once flipped they stay face up in your Fuel zone and keep banking until you spend them. Flip a Fighter or Note instead and it is also face up and playable straight from Fuel, but it fills a Fuel slot until you play it, so a zone clogged with unplayed Fighters can leave you short on spendable Sats.
  • Deploy. Play a Fighter from hand or Fuel, or attach a Gear. Discard Sats equal to its cost. One active Fighter at a time unless a card says otherwise.
  • Attack. Your active Fighter uses its Move. Pay any Fuel cost, then resolve and compare Power against the opponent's active Fighter. Lower Power discards 1 card from the top of its Stack into its Discard. A tie means nothing happens. Play an Energy card to boost the Move before it resolves. If the opponent has no active Fighter, the attack connects automatically.
  • Note. Either player may play a Nostr Note at instant speed, before or right after the Attack's damage is finalized.
  • Pass. The turn passes to the opponent, who starts back at Draw.

On the table, your turn moves one card through these zones: Deck, Fuel, Active, Discard, while Fuel banks and your Stack loses cards when you lose a clash. Your opponent plays the same zones, mirrored on their side. The Pot sits in the middle and goes to the winner.

08 HOW YOU WIN
KNOCKOUT

One path to victory. Drop your opponent's Stack to zero through Fighter clashes. Lose a clash, discard 1 card. Run out and you're out. No life totals to track, ever.

The Pot

Before anyone shuffles, both players put something in the middle: a B collectible, or an agreed amount of Sats. One or the other, never a card from your deck. The Knockout winner takes it all. Play for fun or play for keeps, agree the stakes first.

How to read an icon
  1. The icon badge, just under the art. This one is Hodler.
  2. The Move: what the card does on its own.
  3. The icon rule, printed under the Move. Hodler gives +1 Power for every turn this Fighter stays on the table. No icon, no extra line.

Every icon works on Fighters and Mystery B cards. Icons are entirely optional and random: a card with none plays exactly as its Move describes. Each icon also lists which other card kinds can carry it (Fuel, Note, Gear, Energy, Trap).

13 FAQ

The "wait, can I…?" questions, answered.

Can I play a Mystery B in my deck?
Yes, max 1 per deck, and only if you own one. It never comes from a regular pack, only from a Just Box.
What happens when Power is tied?
Nothing. Nobody discards a card and the game moves on.
Do my Sats run out at the end of the turn?
No. Sats never expire. Once flipped they stay face up in your Fuel zone until you spend them.
I flipped a Fighter or Note as Fuel. Now what?
It sits face up in your Fuel zone and you can play it from there. It uses a Fuel slot until you do, so too many unplayed ones can leave you short on Sats.
Does the first player flip Fuel?
Not on the first turn. The player going first skips their first Fuel flip.
How many Fighters can be active?
One at a time, unless a card says otherwise.
Do Traps count toward my Fighter or copy limits?
No. Run 0 to 2 Traps per deck. A Trap hits you the moment you draw it.
Do I need a rare card to play?
Never. Street cards are always available and every card plays the same. Rarity only changes how many copies exist.
