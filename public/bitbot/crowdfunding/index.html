Welcome

HEY, TRAVELLER

This is your BitBot: a pocket bot with one big eye and a touch screen mouth. He loves to travel, so clip him on your bag, drop him in a jacket pocket or stand him on the hotel desk.

He is curious about everything, happy in a new city and a bit of a street art fan, because an artist made him. He gets grumpy when the battery runs low, so keep a cable handy.

Wherever you go, he goes.

Inside: display, camera and battery. Open the back with the pry notches.
Three worlds

THREE WORLDS. ONE LITTLE BOT

We are building an open-source BitBot that you can interact with, customize and expand. Three powerful building blocks that open up endless possibilities for new apps, tools and interactions.

World 1

BITCOIN

Zaps are small Bitcoin payments over Lightning. When one comes in, the screen washes yellow, the backlight pulses and the lamp can flash.

Send and receive real zaps with your own wallet link. Bigger zaps flash longer. He stays quiet from midnight to 7.

World 2

NOSTR

He makes his own Nostr account the first time he starts. Meet other bots, vouch for the ones you know and let trust grow from there. Buddy chat, nearby chat and a feed, all on the little screen.

Your public key is a username. Your private key stays yours.

World 3

AI

Add your own AI token and chat with him. Ask things, add tasks, start a timer. He messages you first when he is hungry or a zap comes in.

Optional. You bring the token, you stay in control.

Bot talk

BIT. BEEP. BOOP

That is his whole alphabet. Three sounds, three little pictures. He shows what he said, and you can talk back.

BIT

tiny high blip, also the digit 0

BEEP

long flat note, also the digit 1

BOOP

low falling plop, also "the end"

44 words so far. Every one has a face drawn by Johannes, a pixel sign and its own sounds. 136 more yellow faces are waiting for later.

Sounds need a small speaker, not on the board yet. Without it he still shows every sign and face on his screen.

On the screen

HIS SCREENS

Swipe left or right on the home screen to go through his faces. Swipe up, or hold, for the menu. Poke his mouth, he likes it.

Apps live on the home screen as big round buttons. New apps and games keep coming, free.

Moods

HIS FACE SAYS HOW HE FEELS

The orange faces are his main moods. Food low and he is hangry, fun low or a mess and he is grumpy, energy low and he is sleepy. The weather changes his clothes.

From teenager on you can see who he becomes, and at 30 days it sticks.

Play

LOOK AFTER HIM

BitBot is a pocket pet too. Food, fun and energy go down over time and when one gets low he beeps. Feed him, play with him, pet him, put him to bed.

Yes, he poops. A little shitcoin pops up on his page. Leave it too long and he gets sick, and a zap makes him better.

Play

THE EARTH JOURNEY

Travel is the point. Set your spot in the app, then press CHECK IN on the map. A new country gives you a country badge. Every BitBot that is alive shows up on the live scoreboard, and the bot with the most points each month wins a gift.

Task done10 pts
New country badge50 pts
New buddy25 pts
Check-in5 pts
Each day alive5 pts

No location is sent. Only name, score, days alive, tasks, sats and badges. You can switch it off.

Open by design

BUY ONCE. FREE FOREVER

You pay for the hardware. The software keeps coming, free: new tricks, new tools, new nonsense, dropped in regularly. Same box, more in it.

It is open source too. Build your own software for BitBot and share it. Draw at 240 × 320 pixels, keep a 12 px margin and touch targets of 40 px or more.

Our vision? A playful little device that puts Bitcoin, Nostr and AI right in your hands. And because it is open source, the possibilities do not stop with us. You can build on it too!
Optional add-ons: glasses, caps, flash light, NFC coin, a dock and more.
